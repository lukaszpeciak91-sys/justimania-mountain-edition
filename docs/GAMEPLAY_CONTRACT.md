# Gameplay Contract

## Core loop

- Portrait vertical platformer with a continuous upward climb.
- Justyna jumps/bounces automatically upon landing; the player controls horizontal direction only and may redirect while airborne.
- Falling beneath the playable camera area eventually ends the run.
- Score will later be based primarily on maximum achieved height.

## Mountain progression (future)

Checkpoints use real mountain names and elevations, ordered by elevation. Gameplay may compress real-world elevation differences so milestone signs appear at useful intervals. Pixel distance is **never** presented as a literal 1:1 measure of metres.

Cyrla, Jaworzyna Krynicka, Radziejowa, Tarnica, Śnieżka, Babia Góra, and Rysy are a **provisional concept list only**. Their identity, naming, elevation, ordering, and suitability require later research and explicit approval before implementation.

## Future game-over data

Plan for maximum achieved height, highest mountain checkpoint passed, and a locally saved high score. These are contracts for future design, not implemented features.
